What you type

Your salary and every other figure you enter are worked out inside your own browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

So you do not have to start from scratch on your next visit, your entries are saved in your browser's storage on your device. Press Start again to clear them, or clear your browser's site data.

Cookies and adverts

This site sets no cookies. It has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

The plan is to pay for the site with adverts later on. This page will be updated before any advert appears, and you will be asked for your choice first.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it records technical details of each request, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the service running and secure. We do not receive those records.

Your rights

UK data protection law gives you the right to ask what personal data an organisation holds about you and to have it corrected or deleted. We hold none from this site, but you are welcome to ask: support@appsiozo.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
